Web Banking App — Menu & Endpoint Catalog

Visual reference for src/utils/endpoint_catalog.py, used by the endpoint-router specialist in the bank branch officer demo. Each menu item below is backed by the REST endpoint shown. Open a GET item's Show test data panel to see its mock response for the selected customer.

Accounts
  • GET /accounts retail

    List the current customer's accounts with type and balance.

    Accounts > Overview

  • GET /accounts/{id} retail

    Get the details of a single account.

    Accounts > Overview > Account details

  • GET /accounts/{id}/transactions retail

    List recent transactions for one account, with amount/date filters.

    Accounts > Transactions > Search

  • POST /accounts/{id}/transactions/{txn_id}/dispute retail

    File a dispute on a single transaction.

    Accounts > Transactions > Dispute a charge

  • GET /accounts/{id}/statements/{period} retail

    Download a monthly account statement.

    Accounts > Statements

  • GET /accounts/business/overview business

    View a consolidated overview of all business accounts and sub-accounts.

    Accounts > Business > Consolidated overview

Payments
  • POST /payments/transfer retail

    Make a domestic transfer between the customer's own or saved accounts.

    Payments > Transfer money

  • POST /payments/utility-bill retail

    Pay a utility bill by biller code and reference number.

    Payments > Pay a bill

  • POST /payments/external-transfer premium

    Send an international or external-bank wire transfer.

    Payments > International transfer

  • POST /payments/batch business

    Upload a batch payment file, e.g. monthly payroll.

    Payments > Batch / payroll upload

  • GET /payments/scheduled retail

    List scheduled and recurring payments.

    Payments > Scheduled payments

  • DELETE /payments/scheduled/{id} retail

    Cancel a scheduled or recurring payment.

    Payments > Scheduled payments > Cancel

Cards
  • GET /cards retail

    List the customer's debit and credit cards.

    Cards > Overview

  • POST /cards/{id}/block retail

    Block a lost or stolen card.

    Cards > Overview > Block card

  • POST /cards/{id}/unblock retail

    Unblock a previously blocked card.

    Cards > Overview > Unblock card

  • GET /cards/{id}/limits premium

    View a card's current daily spending and withdrawal limit.

    Cards > Overview > View limits

  • PATCH /cards/{id}/limits premium

    Raise or lower a card's daily spending/withdrawal limit.

    Cards > Overview > Change limits

Loans
  • GET /loans/offers retail

    List the loan products and headline rates available to the customer.

    Loans > Offers

  • GET /loans retail

    List the customer's existing loans with balance, installment and maturity.

    Loans > My loans

  • GET /loans/applications/{id}/status retail

    Check the status of a submitted loan application.

    Loans > My applications > Status

  • POST /loans/applications premium

    Submit a new personal loan or mortgage application.

    Loans > Apply

  • POST /loans/business/credit-line business

    Apply for a revolving business credit line.

    Loans > Business > Credit line

Profile & support
  • GET /profile retail

    Get the customer's profile and contact information.

    Profile > My details

  • PATCH /profile/contact-info retail

    Update the customer's phone number or email address.

    Profile > My details > Edit contact info

  • POST /support/tickets retail

    Open a new customer support ticket.

    Support > New request

  • GET /support/tickets/{id} retail

    Check the status of an existing support ticket.

    Support > My requests

General
  • GET /general/conditions retail

    List standard account and product terms, fees, and interest conditions.

    General > Terms & conditions

  • GET /general/branches retail

    List branch locations with address and map coordinates, optionally filtered by city or postal code.

    General > Branch locator

  • GET /general/branches/{id}/hours retail

    Get a branch's opening hours for the week, including holiday exceptions.

    General > Branch locator > Opening hours
